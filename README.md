# Getting Started at NDHU: A Guide for International Students

## 1. Arriving at NDHU

### Getting from Taoyuan Airport to NDHU

1. **Taoyuan Airport to Taipei Main Station:** Take the Airport MRT (NT$160).]

2. **Taipei Main Station to the Hualien area:** Take a Taiwan Railway (TRA) train. Timetable: https://www.railway.gov.tw/tra-tip-web/tip?lang=EN_US

3. **Choose one of two routes:**
   - **Route A:** Get off at Hualien Station, then take Bus 301 (NT$59) to NDHU.  A taxi costs about NT$400-500, which is quite expensive. You can see the approximate time  when a bus runs 

on Taiwan bus app.
   
- **Route B:** Get off at Zhixue Station. Walking to NDHU is 1.5 km, which is not recommended with luggage. A taxi costs about NT$150, but few drivers accept this short trip. [CHECK: 

You can book your train ticket from Taipei to Zhixue Station in OSA Limited Shuttle Bus Timetable app before you come to Taiwan, so you can simply use it on your travel date.  

### What I wish I'd known
For Dorm VII, you need to bring or buy your own bedding: a fitted bedsheet and mattress for a 194 × 82 cm bed, a pillow with a pillowcase, and a blanket. You can buy those at the supermarket outside the university campus. 

### What stressed me the most
Walking to the dorm. The campus is huge, I was carrying two suitcases, and I did not know where the dorm was until my freshman leader sent me the location. I recommend that you contact your freshman leader or Mrs. Jenny beforehand, so you won't wander around carrying your suitcases.

### Tips
- Apply for your ARC (Alien Resident Certificate) as soon as possible in this website: https://coa.immigration.gov.tw/coa-frontend/student/entry?lang=en
- Make sure you have mobile data on your phone so you can find the right place to collect your ARC. If you don't have mobile data yet, prepare clear directions beforehand, or go with a friend instead of going alone. 

You can collect ARC at NIA Hualien County Service Center 

Address: 5F, No. 371, Zhongshan Road, Hualien City, Hualien County 970009, Taiwan.

Opening hours: Monday–Friday, 8:00 AM–5:00 PM, including lunchtime.

- If you plan to use the bus often, buy an EasyCard and then sign up for a monthly bus pass at 7-Eleven. A monthly pass is cheaper than paying for each ride. Each pass is valid for 30 days and gives unlimited rides within its coverage. There are two options:

  | Pass | Price | Coverage |
  |---|---|---|
  | Hualien Basic Monthly Pass | NT$199 | Eligible Hualien local buses, plus Taiwan Railway (TRA) between Heping and Fuli stations on eligible services |
  | Hualien Extended Monthly Pass | NT$399 | Everything in the Basic Pass, plus eligible intercity and highway bus routes within Hualien County and selected Hualien-Taitung services |


## 2. Phone plan

I recommend signing up for the NT$600-per-month contract with Chunghwa Telecom (in my opinion, one of the best telecom companies in Taiwan) on Registration Day. It is a good opportunity to get mobile data without an ARC. If you miss it, you must wait until you receive your ARC and then sign up in the city. Without mobile data, collecting my ARC alone in the city was very difficult.

## 3. Dorm life

Prepare cleaning tools, and agree on a cleaning schedule with your roommates. Your room is where you live and study the most, so keeping it clean matters.

## 4. Food and groceries

**If you want to cook:** Buy food outside the campus, near or inside a supermarket. It is quite a bit cheaper than ready-made food from FamilyMart.

**If you don't want to cook:** Buy a meal box from the fried chicken shop and the other sellers beside it. They are near FamilyMart, between Dorm V and Dorm VI, a short walk from Dorm VII. They sell a variety of foods. I recommend a meal box with vegetables, egg, and the food you want, so you get enough protein and vitamins. Since you will eat there long-term, try not to eat only fried food.
